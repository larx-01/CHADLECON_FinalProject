Our Story

Deep Roots in Kapampangan Culinary Heritage

Chaledcon was born from a generations-old kitchen in Angeles City, Pampanga, where culinary devotion isn't just a hobby—it's a way of life. Named after our beloved grandparents, we preserve the precise culinary customs that make Kapampangan cooking legendary.

Each empanada is meticulously pleated by hand, honoring a sacred symmetry. Our dough is laminated over hours to achieve that iconic crispy-flaky bite, guaranteeing a warm taste of home in every delicious, golden pastry.
